Since 2000

From a small shop to a name families trust

What started as a small dairy shop on New Agraharam Street in Vellore has, over twenty-five years, become a trusted name for pure ghee and traditional sweets. The recipes haven't changed — only the number of families who count on us.

Every tub of khova, block of paneer and jar of ghee still leaves our kitchen made fresh, with honest ingredients and no preservatives. That's the promise behind Trust · Taste · Tradition.
