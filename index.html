Our Pure Cow Ghee is made using the traditional bilona method from the finest quality cow's milk — aromatic, granular and golden. Rich in flavour and slow-cooked in small batches, it carries the same taste families have trusted since 2000.

100% pure cow's milk butter (clarified). No preservatives, no colour, no added flavour.

Per 100g (approx): Energy 900 kcal · Total Fat 100g · Saturated Fat 62g · Cholesterol 256mg · Vitamin A 440µg · Protein 0g · Carbohydrate 0g.

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. No refrigeration required. Best consumed within 9 months of manufacture. Always use a clean, dry spoon.
